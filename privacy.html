Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Photo Recovery Pro ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Photo Recovery Pro does NOT collect, upload, or share your personal files, photos, videos, or contacts with any external servers.

All scanning, indexing, and recovery operations take place exclusively on your local device. Your private media stays on your phone.

3. Device Permissions

To provide file recovery features, the app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Storage / Manage External Storage: Required to locate deleted or hidden photos, videos, and media on device storage.
  • Internet Access: Used solely to load non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements to support app maintenance. Google may collect anonymous device identifiers and analytics according to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository: elhoucein/photorecovery.
